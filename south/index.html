Where the air came from

Each line traces the air that arrived over the city at 11:30 IST five days back in time, along the 850 hPa winds (about 1.5 km up) of NASA's GEOS-FP analysis. Orange lines crossed Punjab, Haryana or Delhi while fields there were burning; blue lines spent most of the five days over the sea. Dots are VIIRS fire detections of the five days before the selected date; the dashed line is a cyclone track.

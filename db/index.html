Burned ground, not just fires seen

Fire counts only see what is burning when a satellite passes. NASA's MODIS burned-area product maps the scars instead, whenever the fire was lit. It is coarse (500 m) and misses many small fields, so its hectares are a floor, but it does not depend on the hour of burning.
